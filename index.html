The Problem

Spain's fastest-growing workforce is its least understood.

Spain absorbed 368,000 new permanent immigrants in 2024 — fifth in the OECD. Yet the institutions responsible for managing integration are flying blind.

  • Municipalities rely on annual national statistics to make decisions that need to be made this week.
  • Employers lose workers to skill mismatches they could not see coming.
  • Incubators demonstrate social impact without the data to prove it.

The people driving Spain's economic growth deserve better. So do the organisations responsible for their integration.
